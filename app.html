Manual entries are marked EST. and carry the method you chose. A saved entry writes one holding and one valuation to the ledger under the source "Manual entry (synthetic)".

A cash flow is money in or out, recorded from the date you choose. It carries the source "Manual entry (synthetic)" and does not change net worth on its own; it shows on the flow side of the wealth series.
